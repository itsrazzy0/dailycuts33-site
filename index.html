Short-form video · every day

The best moments of long videos, cut into clips you actually finish.

dailycuts33 turns long-form video into vertical highlight clips and publishes them daily on TikTok and Instagram — captioned, scored with music and ready to watch in seconds.

Watch on TikTok How it works

What we publish

Every clip is a self-contained moment: a hook in the first second, one idea, and a clear end. Formats are vertical 9:16, usually 20–60 seconds.

Highlight clips

The strongest 20–60 seconds of longer videos, picked for pace and payoff rather than length.

Captioned by default

Large on-screen text keeps clips understandable with the sound off, positioned clear of platform buttons.

App & brand spots

Short promotional cuts for apps and brands, clearly labelled as ads where required (#ad).

Daily cadence

New clips on a steady schedule — one to two per day per platform, never bursts of duplicates.

Built on official platform APIs

Publishing runs through the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram Graph API with the account owner’s explicit authorisation — no scraping, no password sharing, no fake engagement. Details on How it works; what data is touched is in the Privacy Policy.
