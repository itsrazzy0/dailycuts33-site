Workflow

From long video to published clip in five steps.

dailycuts33 combines automated tooling with the editor’s review. This page explains each step and exactly how TikTok and Instagram are connected.

The pipeline

  1. Source

    Long-form videos we are permitted to use (own material and campaign material supplied for clipping) are added to a local library.

  2. Transcribe

    Speech is transcribed on the operator’s own computer with an open-source speech model, so timestamps are known for every sentence.

  3. Find the highlight

    A locally-run language model reads the transcript and proposes the strongest self-contained segments, each with a title and caption.

  4. Edit

    The segment is cut, reframed to vertical 9:16, captioned, and mixed with licensed or royalty-free music and sound effects.

  5. Publish

    Finished clips are scheduled and posted to TikTok and Instagram through their official APIs, with a caption and hashtags. A push notification tells the editor when a clip goes live.

TikTok integration

The dailycuts33 app connects to TikTok in exactly two ways. Both require the account owner to sign in on TikTok and approve the request.

TikTok product / scopeWhat we use it for
Login Kit · user.info.basicThe account owner authorises the app once. We read only the account’s open ID and display name to confirm which account is connected.
Content Posting API · video.publishDirect Post of the finished clip to the connected account, with the caption, hashtags and privacy setting chosen by the account owner.

Owner stays in control

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps, which immediately ends all access.

Only our own account

The app posts to accounts operated by dailycuts33. It has no sign-up for third parties and never posts on behalf of anyone else.

No data resale

Tokens are stored locally and are never shared or sold. See the Privacy Policy.

Instagram integration

Reels are published with the Instagram Graph API using an Instagram professional account that is linked to a Facebook Page. The account owner grants the publishing permission once; the same revocation options apply.

Quality rules we follow

Original edits

Every clip is re-edited — different cut points, captions and music — instead of re-uploading someone else’s video unchanged.

Ads are labelled

Promotional content carries #ad and the brand tags required by the platforms and campaign partners.

No spam

No duplicate posts in the same minute, no bulk following, no automated likes or comments.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use the site?

No. The site is informational; there is nothing to sign up for. The clips are on TikTok and Instagram.

Is my data collected when I watch the clips?

No. We only receive the aggregate statistics TikTok and Instagram show every creator. We do not collect data about viewers or commenters.

Can I request a clip to be removed?

Yes. Email itsrazzy0@gmail.com with the link and we take it down promptly.
