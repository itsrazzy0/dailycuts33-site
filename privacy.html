Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026 Operator Rafael Neufeld Contact itsrazzy0@gmail.com

dailycuts33 is an independent short-form video channel run by a single operator. This website is informational: it has no accounts, sign-ups or logins for visitors. The publishing app behind the channel only acts on the two social accounts the operator owns. This page explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

01

Who this covers

This policy covers data handled by the automation pipeline itself. It does not cover TikTok's or Meta's own handling of data on their platforms — see their respective privacy policies for that.

02

03

What it does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins for anyone other than the operator.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts on any page tied to this project.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.
  • No data from TikTok/Instagram viewers or commenters is collected or stored.
04

Third parties involved

Publishing a clip can involve, depending on the platform:

  • Metricool — schedules and hosts video files briefly for TikTok delivery.
  • YouTube API Services (Google) — for uploading clips to YouTube Shorts the app uses the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload scope only). By using this app with YouTube you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app only uploads videos; it does not read, list or delete existing videos and does not access viewer data. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • ntfy.sh — sends the operator a push notification when a clip goes live. Receives no data beyond a notification topic and message text.
05

Retention & deletion

Access tokens are kept until the operator revokes them from the TikTok or Meta developer dashboard, which immediately cuts off the pipeline's access. Local video files are kept or deleted at the operator's own discretion.

06

Changes

This policy may change as the pipeline changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
