02

What it does

  • Splits source video into short clips using local transcription and a local language model.
  • Renders each clip with burned-in captions, background music, and a vertical crop.
  • Publishes finished clips to @dailycuts33 on TikTok and Instagram on a fixed daily schedule.

All processing runs on the operator's own machine. No clip is published without the pipeline having been configured by the operator in advance.
